# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Two audiences, weighted equally: hiring managers and peers checking Adrian out after LinkedIn or a talk, who need the story to land in under a minute; and fellow builders who enjoy craft and personality.

## Product Purpose

Adrian Popescu's personal portfolio (adrianpopescu.dev). It shows the path from esports to AI-first building and proves that one product owner who still writes code can change what a team ships.

## Positioning

Ten years inside esports (engineer at GosuGamers, admin and referee at The International and Majors), a CS thesis on multi-agent systems long before agents were mainstream, and now applied AI adoption at a German statutory health insurer. Nobody else has that exact path.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Next.js on Bun, TypeScript strict, deployed on the Vercel Hobby tier: keep serverless and edge usage modest, no paid add-ons.
- Homepage is written as release notes (v1.0 esports, v2.0 berlin, v3.0 ai-first).
- The /about page is public: linked in the header menu and listed in the sitemap.

## Brand Commitments

- Workflow Handoff logo (app/layout.tsx): two interlocking hooks carrying one n8n-style run, trigger, handoff, agent, output.
- Interactive particle drawings (Berlin TV tower, esports arena) that scatter from the mouse are part of the site's voice.
- Voice: plain, first person, short sentences, no hype.

## Evidence on Hand

All facts live in app/content.ts and app/page.tsx. Confirmed for the story: first code was written for games; BSc Computer Science at TU Cluj-Napoca with a thesis on multi-agent systems for traffic routing (AI, roughly 15 years ago). No other early-life specifics are confirmed: do not invent PC builds, LAN parties, first games or dates before 2012.

## Product Principles

- Prove, don't claim: every line maps to a real role, project or number.
- Systems other people can run beat demos.
- Play is part of the story, not decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Respect prefers-reduced-motion everywhere (the existing particles and pen trail already do); keyboard reachable; both light and dark themes.
